PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect and handle your personal information.

Effective Date

August 1, 2024

At Gayathri Thread Couture, accessible from https://gayathrithreadcouture.in, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information we collect and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you register or place an order, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information on how the site is accessed and used, including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders and manage your account.
  • To communicate with you regarding your orders, customer support, and promotional materials (if consented).
  • To analyze usage patterns to improve our website and services.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience. You can choose to accept or refuse cookies.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except for necessary service providers or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal information held by us. Contact us via our website for any such requests.

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.
