REFUND POLICY

Refund Policy for Gayathri Thread Couture

At Gayathri Thread Couture, we are committed to ensuring your satisfaction with our products. Please read our refund policy carefully to understand your rights regarding refunds and returns.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

To be eligible for a refund, you must return the item within 7 days of receipt. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and in the same condition that you received it.

2. Non-Refundable Items

  • Customized or personalized items
  • Gift cards
  • Sale items

3. Return Process

To initiate a return, please contact us at gayathrithreadcouture@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return. We will provide instructions on how to return your item.

4. Refund Processing

Once we receive your returned item, we will inspect it and notify you of the status of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 7-10 business days.

5. Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven’t received a refund yet, please check your bank account or contact your credit card company. If you’ve done all this and still have not received your refund, please contact us at support@gayathrithreadcouture.in.

6. Exchanges

If you need to exchange an item for the same product, please contact us at gayathrithreadcouture@gmail.com. Exchanges are subject to availability.

7. Shipping Costs

You will be responsible for paying your own shipping costs for returning your item. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our refund policy, please contact us at gayathrithreadcouture@gmail.com.
