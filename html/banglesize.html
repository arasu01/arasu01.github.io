BANGLE SIZE GUIDE

Find your perfect bangle fit with our comprehensive sizing guide and measurement charts

How to Measure Your Bangle Size

The following chart will help you determine your bangle size exactly. To measure your hand, you will need a measuring tape or a thread and a scale. Bring your thumb and little finger together and measure where your hand is the widest.

Step-by-Step Measurement Guide

  1. Prepare your tools: Get a measuring tape or a thread and a ruler/scale for accurate measurement.
  2. Position your hand: Bring your thumb and little finger together to form the narrowest point of your hand.
  3. Measure the widest part: Wrap the measuring tape around the widest part of your hand (usually across the knuckles).
  4. Record the measurement: Note down the measurement in inches or centimeters.
  5. Find your size: Use the chart below to determine your perfect bangle size.

Measure Your Hand Circumference

Use a measuring tape or string to measure around the widest part of your hand. If using string, mark the length and measure against a ruler.

Pro Tips

For a comfortable fit, choose a bangle that's slightly larger than your hand measurement. This ensures easy wearing and removal.
