Meet Our Team

The artisans and experts behind your beautiful jewelry

Gayathri - Founder & Chief Designer

With over 10 years of experience in traditional silk thread jewelry craftsmanship, Gayathri founded this venture with a mission to make authentic, handcrafted jewelry accessible to everyone. Trained under renowned artisans from Tamil Nadu, she combines traditional techniques with modern design sensibilities to create pieces that honor heritage while appealing to contemporary tastes.

Expertise: Traditional South Indian jewelry design, Bulk customization strategies, Bridal jewelry consulting, Color theory and matching, Quality assurance, Team training and development in traditional craftsmanship techniques.

Vision: "Our goal is to keep traditional silk thread jewelry craft alive while making it relevant and accessible for modern celebrations. Every piece we create tells a story and carries forward the legacy of our artisan heritage."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products and services

How long does it take to create custom jewelry?

Standard orders take 3-5 business days for production plus 3-7 days for delivery depending on your location. Bulk orders (100+ pieces) typically require 7-10 business days for production. For urgent orders, we offer expedited processing at an additional cost. We always communicate realistic timelines upfront and keep you updated throughout the production process.

Do you offer customization for bulk orders?

Absolutely! We specialize in bulk customization with unlimited color options, name/initial embellishments, and design modifications. Our minimum order for full customization is 50 pieces. We provide digital mockups before production and offer sample pieces for approval on large orders. Contact us at +91 9360235889 for a free consultation and to discuss your specific requirements. We've successfully handled orders up to 5000+ pieces for major events.

How do I care for silk thread jewelry?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Avoid contact with water, perfumes, and harsh chemicals as they can damage the silk threads and embellishments. Clean gently with a soft, dry cloth after each use. For detailed maintenance instructions, storage tips, and troubleshooting advice, read our complete Jewelry Care Guide. With proper care, your silk thread jewelry can last for years.

What are your shipping charges and delivery areas?

FREE shipping on all orders above ₹999 anywhere in India. For orders below ₹999, standard shipping charges of ₹50 apply. We ship pan-India to all 28 states and 8 union territories through trusted courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart). Tracking information is provided via SMS and email once your order is dispatched. Typical delivery time is 3-7 business days depending on your location. International shipping is available on request - please contact us for rates and timelines.

What is your return and refund policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy for defective or damaged products. If you receive an item that doesn't meet your expectations due to manufacturing defects, contact us immediately with photos. Customized and personalized orders cannot be returned unless there's a quality issue. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the returned item. Read our complete Return Policy and Refund Policy for detailed terms and conditions.
